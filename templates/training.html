Training

StyleGan2:

As stated prior, to properly train our network, we used StyleGan2's generative adversarial network.

To run the wikiart dataset through our model, we first needed to truncate the images in 3 color channel, 256x256 size images.

Using the built in training function, we trained our network on the dataset for 3000 kimg, which is 3000 instances of a thousand images per tick for a total of approximately 750 ticks. This took 3 days to fully train using 1 GPU.

This training data was then stored in the form of a .pkl network file, which StyleGan2 then used to perform image generation and style mixing.

Below are some visualizations of our mean loss functions for both the Generator and Discriminator networks as a result of training on wikiart. The generator is responsible for creating fake images to try to trick the second network, the discriminator network, which is responsible for classifying style.

This is a graph denoting the mean loss of our generator and discriminator networks per tick.

The generator and discriminator networks gradually converge as tick rate proceeds, stabilizing the training process.
